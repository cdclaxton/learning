The mean of a distribution $p(x)$ can be considered to be the value $M$ that minimises:
$$
\int p(x) (x-M)^2 dx 
$$
and so $E[x] = M$.

If the distance function is instead $|x-M|$ then $M$ is the median.

If the distance function is 0 for an exact match and 1 otherwise, then $M$ is the mode of the distribution.
