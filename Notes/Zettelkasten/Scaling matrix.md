#Graphics 

Scaling matrix in 2D:
$$
SM = \begin{bmatrix}
k_x & 0 & 0 \\
0 & k_y & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
Scaling matrix in 3D:
$$
SM = \begin{bmatrix}
k_x & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & k_y & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & k_z & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Enlargment: $k>1$
Shrinkage: $0 \leq k < 1$

In 2D:
- **Uniform (isotropic) scaling**: $k_x = k_y$
- **Non-uniform scaling**: $k_x \neq k_y$
