#AI

Temperature is one of the [[LLM sampling parameters]]

The greater the temperature, the more unpredictable the output as it introduces randomness (useful for creativity)[Wengrow2026]

Even with a temperature = 0, the output is not constant and it uses **Greedy sampling** (selects the most probable word (assuming there isn't a tie)) [Wengrow2026]

Use a lower temperature for facts and advice [Wengrow2026]
