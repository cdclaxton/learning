#AI 

Techniques:
* Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)
	* Examples of conversations are used to update the internal model to stop it producing undesirable content
* Reinforcement learning
* Direct preference optimisation (DPO)

LLM trained to act as a chatbot could be fine-tuned with dialogues

**Pre-training** -- [[LLM]] is trained on the Internet (or other data)
**Post-training** -- fine-tuning with hand-picked, high-quality data, less data than with pre-training
