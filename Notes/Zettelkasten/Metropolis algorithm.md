#ML #Bayesian 

- Generate samples from $p(\theta)$, typically the unnormalised posterior distribution (likelihood times prior)
- Sample values are generated by a random walk through the parameter space
- Generic Metropolis form of the probability of a move from $\theta_{current}$ to $\theta_{proposed}$:
$$
p_{move} = \min \Big( 1, \frac{p(\theta_{proposed})}{p(\theta_{current})} \Big)
$$
- Using the unnormalised posterior:
$$
p_{move} = \min \Big( 1, \frac{p(D|\theta_{proposed}) p(\theta_{proposed})}{p(D|\theta_{current}) p(\theta_{current})} \Big)
$$
- Accept the move if a random value sampled from a uniform distribution in the range $[0,1]$ is less that $p_{move}$
- Tune width of the proposal distribution so that the acceptance ratio is about 0.5 (can be done automatically)
- Can be inefficient, so look at [[Gibbs sampling]]
