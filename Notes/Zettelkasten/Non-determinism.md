#AI 

An [[LLM]] is dependent on its training data

Some models use randomisation to decide the next word in the SNWP [Wengrow2026]

Calculation of probabilities uses floating-point numbers, which can be inaccurate or vary between machines (servers) for the same model [Wengrow2026]

Small changes in the prompt can yield different results, e.g. due to:
- Capitalisation
- Whitespace
